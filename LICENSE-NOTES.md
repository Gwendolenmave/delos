modify it, and share it — for purposes the licence defines as noncommercial.
You cannot use it commercially without a separate arrangement.

## Why this licence and not an open-source one

Two reasons, stated plainly so nobody has to guess.

**Delos is a personal system that holds personal things** — an assistant's
identity, a record of someone's life, credentials to their accounts. A
permissive licence would invite hosted repackaging, and a hosted Delos is a
different product with a different relationship to its user's data than the
one this project is trying to build.

**This project is maintained by one person.** A licence that invites
commercial adoption also invites obligations that adoption creates. Declining
that up front is more honest than accepting it and failing to meet it.

The trade is deliberate: this is **not** an OSI-approved open-source licence,
and calling it one would be wrong. Source-available and noncommercial is what
it is.

## What this means in practice

- **Personal use** — running Delos for yourself, on your own machine, with
  your own model provider: yes.
- **Reading, studying, learning from the source**: yes.
- **Modifying it for your own use**, including replacing the persona,
  connectors, memory backend or interface: yes — that is the entire point of
  the architecture.
- **Sharing your modifications** noncommercially, keeping the licence and
  notices with them: yes, under the licence's distribution terms.
- **Commercial use** — selling it, offering it as a paid or ad-supported
  service, or using it as part of a commercial product: **not under this
  licence.** Ask the licensor — see [Licensor](#licensor) above.

The licence's own definitions of noncommercial purposes, personal uses,
noncommercial organisations and fair use are in `LICENSE`. Read those rather
than this summary when it matters.

## Your data is not covered by any of this

The licence governs the software. It says nothing about your conversations,
your memory store, your prompts or your credentials, because those are yours
and this project never receives them. See `README.md` for what actually
leaves your machine when you use a remote model provider.
